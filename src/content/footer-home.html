Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between New and Pre-owned games?

New games are sealed, unopened copies straight from the publisher. Pre-owned games are previously played copies that we've inspected, cleaned and tested before reselling at a lower price.

How does the buying process work?

Browse the catalogue, add what you want to your cart, and check out securely. Once your order is confirmed, we pack and ship it straight to your door.

When will my products arrive if I order now?

Most orders are dispatched within 24-48 hours and typically arrive within 3-7 business days depending on your location. You'll get tracking details as soon as it ships.

How does the selling process work?

List the game, console or accessory you want to sell, get a quote, and ship it to us. Once it passes inspection, you're paid out right away.

Do pre-owned products come with a warranty?

Yes — every pre-owned item is tested before it's listed and comes with a limited warranty period, so you can buy with confidence.

At ConsoleVault, we offer a wide selection of new and pre-owned games, consoles and accessories at fair prices. Our simple buying and selling process, competitive pricing and careful quality checks on every pre-owned item are what keep our customers coming back.
