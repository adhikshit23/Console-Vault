Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to ConsoleVault. We deal in pre-owned PC components, gaming consoles, and console hardware. We also provide a marketplace service where users can list their consoles, such as PS5s, for other users to use for an agreed period in exchange for a payment.

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when refunds, cancellations, and returns may be available.

1. Pre-Owned Products

All PC components, consoles, and hardware sold through ConsoleVault are pre-owned/second-hand products, unless specifically stated otherwise on the product listing.

Because these products have been previously used, they may show minor signs of use. The condition, functionality, and known defects of a product will be described in its listing to the best of our knowledge.

2. Eligibility for a Refund

A refund may be requested if:

  • The product received is significantly different from its description or listing.
  • The product arrives damaged and the damage was not disclosed in the listing.
  • The product is not functional upon delivery, where the listing stated that it was in working condition.
  • ConsoleVault cancels an order because the product is unavailable or cannot be provided.

Refund requests should be submitted within 48 hours of receiving the product.

ConsoleVault may request photographs, videos, order details, or other information to verify the issue.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may not be provided when:

  • The buyer changes their mind after purchasing.
  • The product was accurately described in the listing.
  • Damage occurs after the buyer receives the product.
  • The product has been modified, opened, repaired, overclocked, or otherwise altered by the buyer.
  • Damage results from misuse, improper installation, accidents, power fluctuations, liquid damage, or other user-caused issues.
  • The buyer does not report an eligible issue within the specified refund period.

4. Returns

Where a return is approved, the buyer may be required to return the product in the condition in which it was received.

Products should be returned with all accessories, cables, controllers, packaging, and other items that were included with the original order.

If the returned product is materially different from the product originally delivered, ConsoleVault may refuse the refund.

5. Refund Processing

Once an approved return or refund request has been verified, the refund will be processed through the original payment method where possible.

The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or bank.

6. Cancellation Before Dispatch

Orders may be cancelled before the product has been dispatched.

If an order is successfully cancelled before dispatch, the customer will generally receive a full refund, subject to any clearly disclosed transaction or payment-processing charges.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the customer may need to follow the return process described above.

7. Console Listing / Usage Service

ConsoleVault also allows users to list consoles, such as PS5s, so that another user can obtain temporary use of the console for an agreed period in exchange for an agreed payment.

The exact terms of each arrangement, including:

  • Usage period
  • Payment amount
  • Security deposit, if applicable
  • Condition of the console
  • Accessories included
  • Responsibilities of the parties

should be clearly agreed upon before the transaction is completed.

8. Refunds for Console Usage Transactions

If a console cannot be provided to the user as agreed, the user may be eligible for a refund of the amount paid for the unused portion of the agreed usage period.

If the user voluntarily ends the usage period early, a refund will generally not be provided unless the listing or agreement specifically allows early cancellation.

If a console becomes unusable because of a problem that was not caused by the user, ConsoleVault may review the situation and provide an appropriate refund for the affected unused period.

No refund will normally be provided for damage, loss, misuse, or unauthorized modification caused by the user.

9. Disputes

If a buyer and seller/user disagree about a transaction, ConsoleVault may review the available transaction information, listing details, photographs, messages, and other evidence before deciding what action, if any, is appropriate.

ConsoleVault reserves the right to request additional information from either party during a dispute.

10. Policy Changes

ConsoleVault may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

For questions regarding refunds, returns, or cancellations, please contact the ConsoleVault support team through the contact details provided on our website.
