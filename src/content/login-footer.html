Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between new and pre-owned games?

New games are sealed copies. Pre-owned games are inspected, cleaned and tested before being offered at a lower price.

How does selling work?

List your game, console or accessory, receive a quote and ship it to us. Once it passes inspection, you are paid out.

When will my order arrive?

Most orders dispatch in 24–48 hours and generally arrive within 3–7 business days, depending on your location.
